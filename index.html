How to play

Clear three walls of candy bricks without losing all three lives.

  1. Press Start game, then move the paddle with the mouse, touch, ←/→ or A/D.
  2. Bounce the ball into the bricks. Each brick is worth ten points.
  3. Where the ball meets the paddle changes its angle, so use the edges for sharper shots.
  4. Clear every brick to reach the next level, worth 250 points, with a faster ball.
  5. You have three lives. Every missed ball costs one.
  6. Clear all three levels to win. Press P or Space to pause.
